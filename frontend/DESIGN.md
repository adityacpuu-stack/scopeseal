# ScopeSeal interface

A document review workspace for people agreeing on written deliverables. Reading this as an evidence desk, with ENERGY 1 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 1. The decision and its quotations form the focal point. A white paper-like reading area sits beside a compact blue-gray ledger. Deep green marks a satisfied scope and primary actions. Graphite text and restrained rules establish document hierarchy. Georgia titles give agreements the voice of a written record; system sans-serif controls stay familiar. Addresses use monospace only for disambiguation. No decorative illustrations, logos, charts or invented usage statistics. Product name is plain text. Narrow screens stack the ledger below the evidence; intermediate screens keep the criteria and evidence readable without a permanent sidebar. Motion is limited to interactive state feedback and respects reduced motion.
